## Should all three submit?

The official PDF permits teams of **up to five** and requires each student's actual contribution in the final report. It asks for GitHub/GitLab source code, README, a video **under or equal to three minutes**, a real-topic demonstration, evaluation and technical documentation. The PDF does **not** specify whether one team representative submits or each member submits individually in Google Classroom.

**Recommended procedure:** share **one project repository**, **one team final report**, **one video**, and **one validated real-topic run**. If Google Classroom has an individual submission for each student, all three submit the same team links and the individual document for their own name. If the lecturer requests one group submission, designate a single uploader, but explicitly list all three students in the report and submission.
